What the [Mixero](https://mixerobtcmix.com/) bitcoin mixer actually does

The Mixero crypto mixer is custodial. You send coins to a deposit address it generates, it pools them with other people's funds, and later pays the same value minus a fee to addresses you set in advance. What disappears is the on-chain link between the coins you sent and the coins you receive. In standard mode the service relies on CoinJoin-style pooling, so your deposit travels next to transactions sent around the same time and an observer sees a crowd instead of one sender.

The minimum is 0.002 BTC, which keeps dust out of the pool, and the fee is a slider rather than a fixed percentage. Pay more and you jump the queue, pay less and you wait. A built-in calculator shows the exact amount that will land on the other side before you commit, more transparency than the old-school bitcoin tumblers ever offered.

[Mixero BTC mixer](https://mixerobtcmix.com/) or ETH mixer?

Most people still arrive for Bitcoin, but the Mixero Ethereum mixer has quietly become the more interesting half of the service. Ethereum is account-based, so one wallet's whole history sits in plain view. The service treats ETH the way it treats BTC in Advanced Mode: funds pass through Monero and come back as ETH on a new address, so the chain you leave and the chain you arrive on never touch directly.

On the Bitcoin side the [Mixero mixer](https://mixerobtcmix.com/) lets you choose between plain pooling and the deeper routes below. Either way you get back the asset you sent, which saves the extra exchange step people tend to forget about.
